Full-Service HVAC Care for Austin Homes and Businesses

Whether you own a single-family home, a small apartment building, or a commercial space in town, we have the know-how and equipment to handle the full range of heating and cooling work. Our technicians bring years of hands-on experience with central air conditioners, gas and electric furnaces, heat pumps, and ductless mini-splits across the region, and we understand the climate patterns, housing stock, and local code requirements that shape how systems perform here.

Most homeowners we work with need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or regular maintenance. Repairs tend to be quick: a burned contactor, a dirty flame sensor, a slow refrigerant leak, or a worn-out blower motor. Replacements get a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling itself to an early death. Maintenance visits cover coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, which does more than anything else to extend the life of your system.

Local businesses receive the same care. An overheated storefront or a freezing office drives customers and staff away, so our commercial crews focus on fast diagnosis and keep common parts on board for rooftop units, split systems, and ventilation equipment. A few minutes of caution before we arrive can head off a much bigger repair. An iced-over AC should be turned off at the thermostat with the fan set to "on", which lets the coil thaw safely. Running it frozen risks an overflowing drain pan and a damaged compressor. A short-cycling furnace, or any burning smell, means it's time to shut the unit off at its service switch and call us right away.

Keeping Your Austin HVAC System Running Longer

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Take a look at the age of your system. That number tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and is also showing higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored for a season, can take out the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
